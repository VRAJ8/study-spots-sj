On Campus

Tower Lawn

The green heart of campus in front of Tower Hall. Laptop on a blanket, campus Wi-Fi, zero walls.

  • Fresh air

Why it works for studying

On a good day there is no better place on campus to read. Shade from the trees, campus Wi-Fi in range, and the classic SJSU view.

Best for reading and light review; there are no outlets and glare makes long screen sessions hard.

Good to know

  • Bring a blanket or grab a bench.
  • Afternoon shade is on the east side.
  • Laptop at full charge only. There are no outlets.
